Maternal Health Indicators (MHI) Beta Software Version SAS v2025
RELEASE DATE: August 2025
-----------------------------------------
xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx
This version of AHRQ's all-payer software is named MHI Beta SAS QI v2025
xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx

The Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality (AHRQ) announces the release of the AHRQ Quality Indicators (QI) 
software for Maternal Health Indicators (MHI) Beta Version v2025 (v2025 August 2025). 

This is an update to the v2025 SAS QI software to include the MHI module.

- The MHI Beta Software includes eleven measures, plus two sub-indicators:
   • MHI 01 – Inpatient Severe Maternal Morbidity Rate, at Delivery (20 indicators)
   • MHI 02 – Inpatient Severe Maternal Morbidity Plus In-Hospital Mortality Rate, at Delivery (20 indicators plus in-hospital mortality)
   • MHI 03 – Refined Inpatient Severe Maternal Morbidity Plus In-Hospital Mortality Rate, at Delivery, Beta (20 indicators plus in-hospital mortality)
   • MHI 04 – Inpatient Mental Health and Substance Use Disorders Rate, at Delivery, Beta
   • MHI 05 – Inpatient Severe Maternal Morbidity Rate, Delivery through 42 Days Postpartum, Beta (20 indicators)
   • MHI 06 – Inpatient Severe Maternal Morbidity Plus In-Hospital Mortality Rate, Delivery through 42 Days Postpartum, Beta (20 indicators plus in-hospital mortality)   
   • MHI 07 – Refined Inpatient Severe Maternal Morbidity Plus In-Hospital Mortality Rate, Delivery through 42 Days Postpartum, Beta (20 indicators plus in-hospital mortality)
   • MHI 08 – Emergency Department and Inpatient Mental Health and Substance Use Disorders Rate, Days 1 to 365 Postpartum Days, Beta
   • MHI 09 – Emergency Department and Inpatient Encounters for Intentional Self-Harm Rate, Days 1 to 365 Postpartum, Beta
   • MHI 10 – Emergency Department and Inpatient Substance Use Disorders and Accidental Overdose Rate, Days 1 to 42 Postpartum, Beta
   • MHI 10A – Emergency Department and Inpatient Substance Use Disorders Rate, Days 1 to 42 Postpartum, Beta
   • MHI 10B – Emergency Department and Inpatient Accidental Overdose Rate, Days 1 to 42 Postpartum, Beta
   • MHI 11 – Emergency Department and Inpatient Perinatal Mood and Anxiety Disorders Rate, Days 1 to 42 Postpartum, Beta

- All relevant AHRQ QI software and documentation for v2025 can be found on the AHRQ QI website at:
  https://qualityindicators.ahrq.gov/measures/mhi_resources. 

- The AHRQ MHI Beta Software v2025 includes codes based on the fiscal year (FY) 2025 International 
  Classification of Diseases, Tenth Revision, Clinical Modification/Procedure Coding System (ICD-10-CM/PCS).

- The MHI observed rates were developed using multiple years of Healthcare Cost and Utilization Project (HCUP) 
  State Inpatient Databases (SID) and State Emergency Department Databases (SEDD) in the reference population.
  The MHI Beta Software release v2025 utilizes three different reference populations, depending on the indicator.
  MHI 01 - MHI 04 use one year of SID data (2022) in the reference population. SID for the year 2022 were available 
  for 47 states plus the District of Columbia at the time of the development of v2025. MHI 05 - MHI 07 require linked 
  SID data for one year and one quarter (Quarter 1 2021 - Quarter 1 2022) to allow for one year of index deliveries
  (denominator) and 42 days of follow-up to identify severe maternal morbidity events (numerator). Linked SID for the 
  years 2021 and 2022 were available for 21 states at the time of development of v2025. MHI 08 - MHI 11 require linked
  SID and SEDD data for two years (2021 and 2022) to allow for one ear of index deliveries (denominator) and 42 days of
  follow-up to identify behavioral health events (numerator). Linked SID and SEDD for the years 2021 and 2022 were 
  available for 15 states at the time of development of v2025.

- The AHRQ MHI Beta Software v2025 is similar to the area-level QIs, in that the software calculates and 
  outputs observed rates; however, MHI rates are reports as number of events per 10,000 in-hospital stays for
  a delivery rather than using the census population as the denominator. Additionally, MHI 05 - MHI 11 include
  post-delivery discharge encounters in the observed rates

- MHI Beta Software does not include risk adjustment for the v2025 release; however, users can generate observed
 rates by predefined variables (RACECAT, POVCAT, STATE, YEAR, PAYER) with the option for the user to specify a
 user-defined stratum. Software instructions are included in the Quality Indicators Software Instructions
 and Data Dictionary, SAS® QI v2025, which is available at:
 https://qualityindicators.ahrq.gov/Downloads/Software/SAS/V2024/Software_Inst_SASQI_v2025_August_2025.pdf.

The Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality (AHRQ) announces the release of the AHRQ Quality Indicators (QI) 
software for Maternal Health Indicators (MHI) Beta Version v2024 (v2024.0.1, September 2024). 

This is an update to the v2024 SAS QI software to include the MHI module.

- The MHI Beta Software includes three measures:
   • MHI 01 – Severe Maternal Morbidity Rate (20 Indicators)
   • MHI 02 – Severe Maternal Morbidity (20 Indicators) Plus In-Hospital Mortality Rate
   • MHI 03 – Refined Severe Maternal Morbidity (20 Indicators) Plus In-Hospital Mortality Rate, Beta
   
- All relevant AHRQ QI software and documentation for v2024 can be found on the AHRQ QI website at:
  https://qualityindicators.ahrq.gov/measures/mhi_resources. 

- The AHRQ MHI Beta Software v2024 includes codes based on the fiscal year (FY) 2024 International 
  Classification of Diseases, Tenth Revision, Clinical Modification/Procedure Coding System (ICD-10-CM/PCS).

- The MHI observed rates were developed using multiple years of Healthcare Cost and Utilization Project (HUP) 
  State Inpatient Databases (SID) in the reference population. The MHI beta release v2024 utilizes three years
  of data (2019-2021) in the reference population. SID for the year 2021 were available for 48 states at the 
  time of the development of v2024.

- The AHRQ MHI Beta Software v2024 is similar to the area-level QIs, in that the software calculates and 
  outputs observed rates; however, MHI rates are reports as number of events per 10,000 in-hospital stays for
  a delivery rather than using the census population as the denominator.

- MHI Beta Software does not include risk adjustment for the v2024 release; however, users can generate observed
 rates by predefined variables (RACECAT, POVCAT, STATE, YEAR, PAYER) with the option for the user to specify a
 user-defined stratum. Software instructions are included in the Quality Indicators Software Instructions
 and Data Dictionary, SAS® QI v2024, which is available at:
 https://qualityindicators.ahrq.gov/Downloads/Software/SAS/V2024/Software_Inst_SASQI_v2024.0.1_September_2024.pdf.
